# Product

## Register

product

## Users

The Radlabs sales team: 4 people at an early-stage startup in India. They cold-call B2B leads all day, mostly on laptops and sometimes on a phone browser (tap-to-call, WhatsApp). On any screen the job is the same: find the right lead, call them, record what happened, and know who to call next. Leads arrive from Apollo exports, LinkedIn, events and hand-made sheets.

## Product Purpose

An internal CRM that keeps every lead in one place: no duplicates, a clear daily call list, a notes thread and call log per lead, and simple team numbers. It replaces scattered spreadsheets and costs nothing per seat. Success: nobody misses a follow-up, nobody calls a lead a colleague already owns, and the founders see the pipeline without asking.

## Brand Personality

Calm, clear, quick. Plain words a non-native English speaker reads at a glance. The interface stays quiet so the next action is obvious; it never lectures or explains itself.

## Anti-references

- The generic AI / SaaS look: gradient cards, big metric tiles with small labels, coloured side-stripe borders, explanation text wrapped around every section, decorative icons everywhere.

## Design Principles

1. **Next action first.** Every screen answers "what do I do now?" before anything else; secondary actions step back.
2. **Show, don't explain.** Labels and structure carry the meaning; helper sentences only where a wrong move costs data.
3. **Plain words.** Short, everyday wording (match the team's own terms: lead, call, follow-up), no jargon like "map" or "preset" where a simpler word works.
4. **Calm by default, loud only for problems.** Colour and weight are reserved for what needs attention (overdue, needs fixing, do not call).
5. **Trust through transparency.** When the system changes data (imports, bulk edits), show exactly what changed and what did not.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA: text contrast ≥ 4.5:1, visible keyboard focus, tap targets sized for touch on phones, reduced-motion respected. Copy written for non-native English readers.
